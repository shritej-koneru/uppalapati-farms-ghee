How it is made

Milk, curd, and time. Nothing else.

Every jar begins with milk from our own herd, collected the same morning it is churned. We let the milk set naturally into curd, then churn it slowly by hand until the butter separates and the ghee rises clear and golden.

Because the whole process takes most of a day, we work in small batches rather than large ones. It is slower, and it is the reason the aroma tastes like curd rather than butter.

Nothing is added at any stage — no ghee solids, no vegetable oil, no preservatives. What arrives is the same thing we make for our own table.

Each batch is jarred the day it is finished and sealed in food-grade glass, so you can see the colour and grain before you open the lid.
