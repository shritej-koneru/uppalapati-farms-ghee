Verify it yourself

Check the number at the source.

FoSCoS is the government's own compliance system, and searching a registration number there is public — no account needed. You will see the operator's name, the registered address, the kind of business, the validity dates and the product annexure.

If what you find there does not match this page, please tell us. We would rather hear about it than have you quietly decide not to order.

Compare three things: the registration number, the premises address, and the "fee paid upto" date. Those are the ones that decide whether we are trading legitimately today.

Something look wrong? FSSAI's helpline is 1800112100.
