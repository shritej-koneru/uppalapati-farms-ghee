Why it is a registration

Not a licence, and we will not pretend otherwise.

Under the FSS Act, 2006, a food business with annual turnover up to ₹1.5 crore is issued a registration. A full licence is for larger businesses. Ours is a registration, and this page uses that word throughout.

We think that distinction is worth being plain about. A lot of small producers describe a registration as a "licence", and customers are left unable to tell which one they are buying from. You can check ours either way — the number is public.

It was issued on 6 July 2026 as a Tatkal (instant) registration, and the annual fee is paid up to 5 July 2031.

What we are bound by

The rules that come with it.

Is this based on an inspection?

No. A Tatkal registration is issued immediately on the basis of the applicant's self-declaration, without a pre-registration inspection. That is the normal route for a business at our size, but it does mean the certificate reflects our own declaration rather than an officer's visit.

What happens if the annual fee is not paid?

Missing the due date suspends the registration. Payment within 180 days of the due date will usually reactivate it, with any applicable penalty. Beyond 180 days the certificate is treated as cancelled and a fresh application is required. We keep the date visible above so you can see it is current.

What are the consequences of a false declaration?

The certificate carries a penalty of up to ₹10 lakhs under Section 61 of the FSS Act, 2006, for a false declaration. The registration is also for the registered premises only, and may not be used to claim ownership of the premises.

Can this registration be used for anything else?

No. It exists to carry on a food business and for nothing else. It is not a hygiene rating, not a quality award, and not a substitute for batch-level lab testing.

How do I check that this is real?

Use the number 20126121000520 on FoSCoS, the Food Safety Compliance System run by FSSAI. It is a public search and does not need an account. The certificate PDF is also published on this page so you can read it end to end.

Verify it yourself

Check the number at the source.

FoSCoS is the government's own compliance system, and searching a registration number there is public. You will see the food business operator's name, the registered address, the kind of business, the validity dates and the product annexure — the same material reproduced above.

If what you find there does not match this page, please tell us. A mismatch would be our error, and we would rather hear about it than have you quietly decide not to order.

What to compare: the registration number, the premises address, and the "fee paid upto" date. Those three are the ones that matter for whether we are trading legitimately today.

If something looks wrong, FSSAI's national helpline is 1800112100.
